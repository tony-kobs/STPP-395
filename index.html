About the Game

A puzzle built for calm, focused thinking

Hexa Diamonds challenges players to place diamonds, squares, and triangular gems onto a hexagonal grid, rewarding careful spatial planning over speed. Guided by a magical puzzle companion, players work through progressively intricate layouts designed to sharpen logic while staying relaxing and low-pressure. The atmosphere feels elegant, meditative, and visually polished.

How to Play

Simple placement, deep strategy

  • Study the Grid

    Examine the hexagonal board before placing pieces.

  • Place Gem Shapes

    Fit diamonds, squares, and triangles into open spaces.

  • Clear Full Rows

    Complete lines to clear space and score points.

  • Plan Ahead

    Anticipate upcoming shapes to avoid dead ends.

  • Progress Through Puzzles

    Advance through increasingly complex hexagon layouts.
